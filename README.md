# PFE(Workerz)

During these difficult times caused by the Covid, thousands of people are looking for independent workers either to save money or to get help, or both. My goal is to design a website where independent workers and small businesses can sell their products and services. After registering, independent workers can add their personal information, create their own page on the site, and connect with potential clients. Clients can view the independent worker's information on their page and contact them directly.

My second objective, which is equally important, is to allow clients to create job postings that only independent workers can see.

I believe that Workerz will be a success because users can quickly access necessary information and the site is easy to use.

## Why Workerz?

The goal is to support independent workers, especially during this crisis when they face emotional and physical challenges. Workerz will help them find work and help clients find the best independent worker for their needs.

Here are a few examples of existing websites:

- [GrafterNow](https://www.grafternow.com/find-workers/): In this site, I appreciate that users can search for independent workers by zip code (which is logical), but it's a shame that the latest jobs are displayed in a tab instead of being filtered.

- [GigSmart](https://gigsmart.com/find-workers/): I like the ability to find work through a tab. This feature is what made me appreciate this site.

- [Outsourcely](https://www.outsourcely.com/startups-and-businesses): I like that they present categories and allow clients to select an independent worker's occupation. However, the homepage is too long and should focus on essential information. Additionally, there is a cost to register, which is not ideal during this time.

## Target Audience

The target audience is adults and people who need help with small jobs.

## Context

I know several independent workers who are struggling to make ends meet during this Covid crisis, and their situations are getting worse over time.

That's why I decided to design a website that sells independent workers' services and products without any registration fees.

## Users

Users can view all registered independent workers and create job postings after registering.

## Independent Workers

Independent workers can register and provide all necessary information to be visible to potential clients on the website.
